Frosty Jelly Pops

CasualFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Hold to pour jelly layers to the line, freeze them and pop out a striped treat.

About the game

Look at the order card to see the striped jelly pop to make. Hold your finger down to pour each jelly color into the mold and let go right on the dotted line. When every layer is in, hold to freeze it, then hold again to squeeze the mold off and reveal a wobbly striped pop. Stop exactly on the lines to earn three stars.

  • Hold to pour, release on the line
  • Freeze and squeeze reveal
  • Six fun pop molds
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
