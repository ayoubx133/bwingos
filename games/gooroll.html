Swipe the slime and cover every tile of the maze in gooey slime!

About the game

Swipe to send the little slime sliding until it bumps into a block or a candy fence. It leaves a trail of goo on every tile it slides over. Some tiles need two coats, swirl pads zap the slime across the maze, and on twin levels two slimes slide together with one swipe. Cover the whole floor to win.

  • Simple swipe controls and squishy slime fun
  • Every maze can always be finished
  • Six slime colors to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
