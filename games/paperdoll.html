Cut out a paper doll along the dots, color her in and fold on cute accessories.

About the game

Trace the dotted line with your scissors to cut a paper doll out of the sheet. The closer you stay to the dots, the better your stars. Then pick paint buckets and tap the hair, dress and shoes to color her just like the order card. On later levels, drag paper crowns, bows, wands and more onto the doll and watch the tabs fold into place.

  • Snip along the dotted line
  • Tap to fill colors
  • Fold on paper accessories
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
