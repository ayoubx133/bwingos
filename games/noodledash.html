About the game

Hold your finger on the screen to make the bendy noodle run down the kitchen table. Let go to stop and wait on the green tiles while rolling pins, swinging spatulas and squishy marshmallow stompers go by. Time your dash and reach the yummy plate at the end with all your hearts. Earn coins and unlock fun noodle skins like Spinach, Beet and Chef.

  • Simple one finger hold and release controls
  • Soft and silly kitchen obstacles with cute faces
  • Unlock colorful noodle skins with coins
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
