About the game

Every level brings a photo of the dish a customer wants. Drag the food onto the plate where the faded picture shows. Then drag the squeeze bottle along the dotted line to drizzle the sauce, and shake sprinkles inside the circle. Take a photo and see how many stars your pretty plate earns.

  • Hands on plating with your finger
  • Pancakes, sushi, cake, pasta and more
  • Unlock fancy plates to serve on
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
