About the game

Swipe left and right to guide your cute worm down the garden path. Eat colorful berries to grow longer and pick the best gate: SPLIT turns you into two or three worms, while plus gates make every worm longer. Steer around wobbly jelly blocks that nibble segments away. At the end your worms wiggle into giant apples, and the longer they are, the more apples they fill.

  • Simple swipe steering for kids
  • Split into up to three happy worms at once
  • Unlock cute worm skins like Mint, Tiger and Rainbow
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
