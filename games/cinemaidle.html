About the game

Drag anywhere to walk your little usher around the cinema. Grab fresh popcorn from the machine and fill the snack counter so the animal guests can buy a treat. When a movie hall is full the show starts and ticket coins pour out at the door. Collect the coins and pay for new halls and a soda machine to grow your cinema.

  • Easy one finger walking
  • Cute animal guests and real movie shows
  • Unlock new halls, machines and cinemas
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
