About the game

Swipe left and right to steer your sled down the snowy hill. Slide past waiting kids and they hop onto new sleds behind you, making a long and happy sled train. Dodge trees, rocks and snowmen that bump riders off, and hit the ramps to jump the whole train over rock walls. At the bottom, more riders slide further into the bonus zone for bigger rewards.

  • Easy swipe steering for little hands
  • Grow a long sled train full of cheering kids
  • Jump ramps and unlock colorful sleds
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
