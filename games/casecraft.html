About the game

A customer brings a plain phone case and a wish card. Pick a spray can and drag to paint the case, lay a stencil and spray through it, then peel it off for a fun reveal. Drag stickers anywhere you like and shake on sparkly glitter. Match the wish card to earn three stars.

  • Stencils, stickers and glitter to mix and match
  • Unlock cool spray can designs
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn

  • Plays fully offline
