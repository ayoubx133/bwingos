Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Drop blocks of colored sand, watch them crumble and make rainbow bands vanish!

About the game

Blocks made of colorful sand fall into the sandbox. Drag to move a block and tap to turn it, then watch it crumble into thousands of pouring sand grains when it lands. Build a band of one color that reaches from the left wall to the right wall and it disappears with a sparkle. Clear the target number of bands before the sand reaches the top.

  • Relaxing falling sand physics
  • Easy drag and tap controls with a landing guide
  • Unlock sand colors like Ocean, Neon and Sunset
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
