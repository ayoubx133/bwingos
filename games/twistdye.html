Twist, dye and unfold colorful tie dye shirts for every order!

About the game

Pick a fold and fold the white tee with your finger: draw circles for a spiral, swipe for stripes or pull it up for a bullseye. Rubber bands snap on, then hold the dye bottles over each section to soak it with color. Swipe to open the shirt for a big reveal and see how well it matches the order.

  • Fold, dye and reveal real tie dye patterns
  • Spiral, stripes and bullseye designs
  • Unlock tanks, hoodies, crop tops and dresses
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
