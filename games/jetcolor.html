Jet Color Dash

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Fly your jetpack through doors that match your color and splash new paint!

About the game

Hold your finger on the screen to fire up the jetpack and fly up, let go to float back down. Every gate has doors in different colors, and you can only fly through the door that matches your suit. Splash through a paint bubble to change your color before the next gate, but watch out for the wrong color bubbles. Reach the finish line with your hearts to win the level!

  • One finger hold to fly controls
  • Color matching gates and paint splash bubbles
  • Six jetpack and helmet styles to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
