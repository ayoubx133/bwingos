Snowball Hide Out

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pop up from your snow fort and toss snowballs at snowmen and balloons!

About the game

Hold your finger on a snowman or a floating balloon to pop up from behind your snow wall and throw snowballs at it. When a thrower snowman winds up and the alert shows, let go to duck behind the wall and stay safe. Knock over every snowman in the field to win the level. Later levels bring sledding snowmen, sneaky throwers that hide behind snow piles and lots of balloons!

  • One finger hold and release controls
  • Cute winter field with silly snowmen and balloons
  • Six cozy winter outfits to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
