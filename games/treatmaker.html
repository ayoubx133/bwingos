About the game

A customer shows the treat they want. Pick the batter and hold to pour it up to the line, then tap when the oven timer is in the green zone. Drag to pipe swirly frosting and drop on cherries, berries, candles or sprinkles. The closer your treat looks to the order, the more stars you earn.

  • Unlock cute baking cups and trays
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
