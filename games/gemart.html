About the game

Each level shows a picture grid full of numbered dots. Pick a gem color from the pots and drag across the board to drop gems on every dot with the same number. When the picture is full, roll the press over the gems to make them shine. Reveal hearts, kittens, ducks, cupcakes and many more cute pictures.

  • Drag to drop sparkly gems
  • Color by number pictures
  • Six gem shapes to collect
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
