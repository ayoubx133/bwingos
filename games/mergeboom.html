Merge fizzy candy bombs into bigger ones and pop the wobbly candy castle!

About the game

Tap the fizz machine to get little candy bombs. Drag two bombs with the same number onto each other to merge them into a bigger, fizzier bomb. When it is big enough, drag it up onto the wobbly candy castle and watch the blocks pop and tumble. Pop every block before your fizz runs out to win the level!

  • Simple drag to merge and drag to pop controls
  • Tough striped and chocolate blocks in later castles
  • Six sweet bomb skins to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
