Match gems to blast stars at a friendly robot and break its shield first!

About the game

Swipe a gem to swap it with its neighbour and make a row of three or more. Every gem you match sends a shooting star at the robot's shield. Then the robot takes its turn and tries to hit your shield back. Match four for a line gem, five for a star bomb, and empty the robot's shield first to win.

  • Turn based match 3 duels
  • Line gems, star bombs and big combos
  • A cute robot that gets smarter level by level
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
