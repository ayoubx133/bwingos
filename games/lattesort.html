About the game

Holders full of colorful drinks wait on the cafe lanes. Tap a lane to send its front holder to the counter, and the cups hop onto the order trays of their color. A full tray gets served and a new order arrives. Counter spots are limited, so choose the order of your taps with care and keep the cafe running.

  • Cozy cafe sorting with one tap controls
  • Every level is checked to be solvable
  • Six cute drink styles to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
