About the game

Cute animal customers bring a recipe card to the potion shop. Drag the ingredients into the cauldron in the right order, then stir in big circles with your finger and watch the colors swirl together. Heat it up and let go in the green, then hold to fill the bottle right up to the line. Follow the recipe closely to earn all three stars.

  • Stir, heat and pour with real finger moves
  • Bubbly cauldron with swirling colors
  • Unlock six magical cauldrons
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
