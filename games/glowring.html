About the game

A glass ring spins above your little lamp buddy. Tap to shoot a bead of light up at the ring and light the part it hits. Watch out, hitting a part that already glows ends the round. Light every part to wake up the sleepy star in the middle.

  • One tap timing fun for all ages
  • Rings speed up, slow down and turn around
  • Six bright glow colors to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
