About the game

Tap a tube tile to turn it and connect it to the power plug. Every tube that joins the power glows in bright neon colors. Link all the tubes and the whole sign flickers on with a happy buzz. Signs grow from tiny shapes to big hearts, stars and cats as you go.

  • Simple tap to turn controls
  • Every sign can always be solved
  • Unlock six glowing neon colors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
