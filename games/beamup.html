About the game

Hold your finger on the screen to switch on the tractor beam and drag to fly your little UFO over the farm. Hover over chickens, pumpkins and sheep and watch them float up the beam. Every catch makes the UFO bigger, so soon it can lift cows, cars and even the big red barn. Beam up enough before the clock runs out to clear the level!

  • Easy hold and drag controls for all ages
  • Grow from tiny saucer to giant spaceship
  • Unlock six colorful UFO skins
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
