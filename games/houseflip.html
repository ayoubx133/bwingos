Fixer Upper Pals

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Scrub, fix, paint and decorate messy rooms into cozy dream homes!

About the game

A cute customer shows you a wish card for their messy room. Drag the sponge to scrub away the dirt, tap the hammer to fix the cracked floor boards and roll fresh paint over the wall in the color they asked for. Then drag the furniture onto the dotted spots and snap a before and after photo. Match the wish card to earn all three stars.

  • Real scrubbing, hammering and paint rolling with your finger
  • Before and after photo at the end of every room
  • Bedrooms, living rooms and kitchens to make over
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
