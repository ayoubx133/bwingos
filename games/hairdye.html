Streaky Hair Dye

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Brush on bright dye streaks, wrap them in foil and rinse for a colorful reveal.

About the game

Every client brings an order card with the streaky hair look she wants. Pick a dye bowl and drag the brush along her long hair strands to paint the streaks. Wrap the dyed strands in foil, wait for the color to set, then spray the shower over the foil to rinse and reveal the shiny new look. Match the order card closely to earn three stars and unlock new clients.

  • Paint real streaks with your finger
  • Foil wrap and rinse reveal
  • Six cute clients to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
