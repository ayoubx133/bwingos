About the game

Every client wants a look for a theme like beach day, snow day, party time, sports day or a rainy day. For each part, drag one of the choices onto the model: hat and hair, top, bottom, shoes and a fun extra. Some clients also want a favorite color. Then strike a pose, the camera flashes and the judges show their scores.

  • Drag and drop dress up with five fun themes
  • Photo flash and cute judges with score cards
  • Unlock new hair colors
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
