Mix, grind and press pretty powder shades to fill a makeup palette!

About the game

Each order card shows a palette of pretty blush shades. Tap the pigment jars to drop scoops of powder into the bowl, then hold the bowl to grind it smooth and see how close your color is. Hold to press the powder into its pan and let go in the green zone. On later levels finish every pan with a sparkly shimmer stamp.

  • Mix real colors and see the match score
  • Grind, press and stamp with satisfying sounds
  • Six cute compacts to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
