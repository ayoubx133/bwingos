About the game

Press and hold to make the little gymnast jump high off the trampoline and tuck into a spin. Let go to open up and land on your feet. Clean landings make the next bounce even higher, so you can try double and triple flips. Three friendly animal judges hold up their score cards, so reach the target score before the routine ends!

  • Simple hold and release flip controls
  • Bigger bounces and double flips as you improve
  • Six bright gym outfits to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
