Turn the round maze and roll the little balls out into the jar!

About the game

A round maze is full of colorful little balls. Drag around it to turn the whole maze and gravity rolls the balls down. When a gap turns under them they drop to the next ring, and the exit gate lets them roll down into the jar. Fill the jar to the line before the clock runs out.

  • Smooth turning controls with real rolling balls
  • Every maze is tested so the balls can always get out
  • Six fun ball styles to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
