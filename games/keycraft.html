About the game

Build your own clicky keyboard step by step. First pick a spray can and drag to paint the case in the color from the order card. Next choose keycap colors and tap or drag over the sockets to drop each cap into the pattern. Finally tap the letters to type the word on the screen and enjoy every satisfying click.

  • Spray paint the case
  • Drop keycaps in color patterns
  • Type words with clicky sounds
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
