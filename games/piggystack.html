Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Tap to drop swinging piggies and stack the tallest, wobbliest piggy tower!

About the game

A cute piggy swings from the crane above your tower. Tap at the right moment to drop it so it lands on the piggy below. Land it in the middle for a PERFECT drop, but if it hangs too far over the edge it slides off and you lose a heart. Stack piggies all the way up to the GOAL flag to finish the level.

  • One tap timing fun for all ages
  • Silly oinks, wobbles and perfect drop streaks
  • Unlock piggy skins like Spotty, Unicorn and Royal
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
