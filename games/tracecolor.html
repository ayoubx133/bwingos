Trace the dotted carnival pictures, then color them just like the card!

About the game

Follow the dotted lines with your crayon to draw a fun carnival picture like a balloon, an ice cream or a circus tent. Every dot you pass lights up, and staying on the line earns more stars. Then pick the crayons and scribble inside each shape to color it like the sample card. A shape fills up with a happy splash when it is mostly colored.

  • Trace and color with your finger
  • Carnival pictures that get trickier
  • Crayons, markers, pencils and glitter pens
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
