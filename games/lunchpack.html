Make the sandwich, cut it and fit all the food into the lunch box!

About the game

A hungry kid shows you a wish card. Stack the sandwich fillings in the right order and add the top bread, then drag the knife along the dotted line to cut it in half. Now drag every food piece into the lunch box so it all fits perfectly, and tap a piece to turn it. Pack exactly what was wished for to earn three stars.

  • Satisfying fit it all in puzzles
  • Build and cut your own sandwich
  • Six cute lunch boxes to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
