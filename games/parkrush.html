Tap the cars in the right order and drive every one out of the jammed lot!

About the game

The parking lot is packed and every car has an arrow on its roof. Tap a car and it drives out the way the arrow points, then zooms around the road to the exit gate. If another car is in the way it bumps back and you lose a heart, so look before you tap. Clear the whole lot, and pick up the friends waiting at the bus stop when their bus leaves.

  • Easy tap controls with clever traffic puzzles
  • Every lot can always be cleared
  • Six cool car styles to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
