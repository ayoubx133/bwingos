About the game

Drag your finger to steer the little drill and dig tunnels through dirt, clay and rock. Collect shiny gems along the way, and when your fuel gets low, drive back up to the surface shop. Sell your gems to buy a stronger drill that breaks harder rock or a bigger fuel tank, then dig deeper. Reach the treasure chest at the bottom to finish the level.

  • Simple drag controls to dig in any direction
  • Collect gems and upgrade your drill and fuel tank
  • Unlock colorful drill skins like Cherry, Mint and Gold
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
