Number Chain Merge

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Drag a chain of matching numbers and merge them into bigger and bigger tiles!

About the game

Drag your finger across touching tiles with the same number to link them. After the first pair you can also link the next double, so 2, 2, 4, 8 makes one big tile. Let go and the chain merges into a new number with a happy pop. Reach the goal number on the card at the top to finish the level.

  • Smooth drag to link number chains
  • Never stuck: the board shuffles when no chain is left
  • Six fun tile shapes to unlock
  • Unlock fun skins with the coins you earn
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
